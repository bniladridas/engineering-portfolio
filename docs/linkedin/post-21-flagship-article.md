Over the past few years I've found myself thinking less about how quickly software can be built and more about how well it can be maintained.

That led me to write *Building Software That Lasts*, a reflection on configuration design, documentation, incremental change, and the engineering decisions that make systems easier to evolve over time.

The article draws on lessons from projects I've worked on and contributions to open source. I hope it's useful to anyone interested in building software that's intended to be understood as well as used.

For anyone who would like to read more, I also keep longer-form engineering notes on my website.

https://bniladridas.github.io/engineering-portfolio/articles/building-software-that-lasts
