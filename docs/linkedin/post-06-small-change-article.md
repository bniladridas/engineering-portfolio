I used to think a skilled engineer was someone who landed large, ambitious changes in a single move.

I was measuring the wrong thing. The engineers I respect most ship the same amount of work as everyone else, in pieces small enough that nothing can break quietly.

*The Art of the Small Change* is out. The habits that matter:

- One concern per commit. If it needs two paragraphs in the message, split it.
- Structure before behavior. Land the structural half first, alone.
- Every commit leaves the codebase in a working state: that's what makes it safe to revert and bisect.
- Reviewers read small diffs closely and large diffs skimmingly. Small diffs buy real feedback.

Small changes build trust. Trust makes change cheap. Cheap change is the entire game.
