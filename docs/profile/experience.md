# Experience

**Format:** Most recent first. One project-focused bullet per role, one engineering-principle bullet per role.

**Template per role:**
- **Title** — Company, Dates
- First bullet: the biggest project, what it did, what it delivered.
- Second bullet: an engineering decision or practice the role demonstrates (maintainability, docs, reviews, tooling).
- Third bullet (optional): an outcome measured in real terms — fewer incidents, faster onboarding, a tool that removed a recurring task.

**Guidance:**
- Write the bullets as facts, not adjectives. "Designed the auth service used by all 40k users" beats "responsible for authentication."
- Every role should demonstrate at least one of the profile's themes: maintainable systems, developer tools, open source, communication.
- Gaps are fine. LinkedIn doesn't need month-by-month coverage; it needs honest coverage.
