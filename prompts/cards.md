# Card Prompts

Each card is a wide 3:4 or 16:10 image. The design system keeps the card white with black text; the *image* can carry a subtle, structured motif — never a second color.

## 1. Building Software That Lasts

> Flat vector illustration of a brick wall being carefully built where the mortar forms a subtle grid, a single green brick near the center, the rest in warm grey and black line work.

## 2. Learning Through Open Source

> Flat vector illustration of a small pull request branch merging into a main line, the merge point highlighted with a single green dot, monochrome black and grey line work.

## 3. Engineering in Practice

> Flat vector illustration of layered abstract architecture — a stack of clean, aligned blocks with a single green seam running vertically through them, black and grey line work.

## 4. Palmshed

> Flat vector illustration of a small tool shed rendered as clean geometric shapes, a palm leaf growing from its roofline, the leaf in muted green, everything else black and grey line work.

## 5. Documentation as a Deliverable

> Flat vector illustration of an open book whose pages transform into a clean architectural blueprint grid, one green annotation line marking a key detail, black and grey line work.

## 6. Maintainable Systems

> Flat vector illustration of concentric clean boundary lines shielding a small structured core, the innermost line in muted green, black and grey line work, generous whitespace.

## 7. Refactoring Is a Letter

> Flat vector illustration of an envelope composed of geometric rectangles that gently reorganize into a cleaner stack, one green stamp, black and grey line work.

## 8. The Small Change

> Flat vector illustration of a single small stone dropped into still water, the ripple drawn as concentric 12-grid lines, a green dot at the impact point, black and grey line work.

## 9. Auth Done Right

> Flat vector illustration of a simple door with a large, calm keyhole, an outline lock rendered in careful geometric strokes, a single green key, black and grey line work.

## 10. Reading Code, On Purpose

> Flat vector illustration of an open book whose pages contain a minimal network of connected nodes and lines, one node highlighted in green, black and grey line work.

Add the style anchor from `README.md` to every prompt. Export at 2x, confirm the green is exactly `#1f883d`.
