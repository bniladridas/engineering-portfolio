# Diagram Prompts

Conceptual article imagery — not technical diagrams (use the SVGs in `assets/diagrams/` for those), but visual metaphors.

## 1. Interfaces outlast implementations

> Flat vector illustration of a stable stone arch bridge in black and grey line work, with modern machinery quietly being rebuilt underneath it, a single green rivet on the bridge.

## 2. Small changes compound

> Flat vector illustration of a single green seedling growing into a structured tree whose canopy forms a clean grid, black and grey line work, generous whitespace.

## 3. Reading code with intention

> Flat vector illustration of a magnifying glass resting over a map of connected nodes, the focused node highlighted in green, black and grey line work.

## 4. Building in public

> Flat vector illustration of a workshop with its doors wide open, tools drawn in clean geometric strokes, one green tool on the bench, black and grey line work.

Add the style anchor from `README.md` to every prompt.
