# Your Article Title

**How to use this template:** copy this file into `docs/articles/`, rename it to match the slug, and fill in every section. Delete sections you don't use. Keep the voice: short sentences, concrete, no filler, claims demonstrated rather than asserted.

## Opening

The first two paragraphs make the promise. State the thesis plainly, in the first sentence if you can. The reader decides in ten seconds whether to continue.

## The observation

What did you notice that made this worth writing? Specifics beat abstractions: name the moment, the project, the mistake.

## The argument

One idea, developed honestly. Use the tension: what everyone does, and what works better. Concede the counterpoint; it makes the recommendation credible.

## In practice

The actionable part. Concrete rules, patterns, or a short list that the reader can apply this week. This is what gets shared.

## The reflection

What changed in how you work because of this? End with a line that echoes the thesis: no summary, no new ideas.

## Takeaway

Three sentences max. State what the reader should do, and why it compounds.
